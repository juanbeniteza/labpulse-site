Privacy Policy

LabPulse: Homelab Monitor · Effective November 24, 2026

Short version: LabPulse does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data. Everything you set up stays on your device.

What LabPulse stores, and where

  • Your targets (names, addresses, ports, Proxmox node names, settings) and their check history (status and latency for up to 30 days) are stored locally on your device, shared only between the LabPulse app and its widgets.
  • Credentials you enter (Proxmox API tokens, HTTP authorization headers) are stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • None of this is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Network connections

LabPulse connects only to the addresses you configure (for example your Proxmox server or self-hosted services), directly from your device. There is no LabPulse server, account, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service, and the app contains no third-party SDKs.

iOS asks for Local Network permission because checking devices on your home network requires it.

Notifications

If you turn on down alerts, notifications are created locally on your device. No push notification service is used.

Backups

Exporting a configuration backup creates a JSON file that you choose where to save or share. Backups never include API tokens or passwords.

Tracking

LabPulse does not track you across apps or websites and does not use advertising identifiers.

Children

LabPulse does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/juanbeniteza/labpulse-site/issues.
