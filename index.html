LabPulse: Homelab Monitor

Uptime & Proxmox widgets for iPhone

Free No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no cloud relay.

LabPulse checks your self-hosted services and Proxmox VE nodes directly from your iPhone, over your home network or VPN (Tailscale, WireGuard), and shows their health in the app and in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

  • HTTP/HTTPS and TCP checks with latency history
  • Proxmox VE nodes: CPU, memory, uptime and VM/LXC status
  • Watch important VMs and containers and get notified when something goes down
  • Away mode: pauses home-only targets when you leave, so you never get false alerts
  • Read-only: it never starts, stops or reboots anything
